Good to know

  • Local inference runs on the CPU; a discrete GPU is not required.
  • Large language models need free RAM. 16 GB is recommended for comfortable use.
  • Arabic narration requires an Arabic SAPI voice installed in Windows.
  • Bundled model files are licensed separately. See the notices included with the release.
